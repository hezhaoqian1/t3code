# Company Report Writing

## Workflow

1. Read `knowledge/business-rules.md`, relevant files in `knowledge/`, and `templates/management-report.md`.
2. Identify the audience, reporting period, decision to support, and required format.
3. Verify every numeric claim against an approved query result or supplied source file.
4. Separate facts, calculations, interpretations, hypotheses, recommendations, and unknowns.
5. Use the management report template unless the user specifies another approved format.
6. Save the final report to `outputs/` and identify source files, query records, reporting period, and known limitations.

## Quality Rules

- Lead with the decision-relevant conclusion.
- Include comparison bases and units for changes.
- Do not invent missing numbers, causes, targets, owners, or deadlines.
- Do not claim causality from correlation alone.
- Flag conflicting definitions and ask the responsible owner to resolve them.
- Keep sensitive detail to the minimum needed for the audience.
